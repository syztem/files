02
Live-fire procedure
Memorise the sequence; the range officer will not repeat it

    

      
01
Slew the carriage
A / D or the arrow keys walk the hull across the heightmap. Track links run against the ground, the suspension articulates over each crest, and the dust behind the tracks tells you which way you are going.

      
Lay the gun
Move the pointer anywhere inside the viewport and the barrel follows it; the dotted arc is your firing solution, integrated forward with gravity and crosswind factored in. W / S give you fine elevation when the arc is doing something you disagree with.
02

      
03
Fire and hold
Space or a click releases the round. The carriage recoils, the muzzle blast kicks dust off the deck, and the impact craters the terrain — permanently. Long shots need elevation: the arc flattens close, drops hard past mid-range.

      
Survive the exercise
The column returns fire. R relays the magazine, hull integrity does not. Should the carriage burn out, Enter redeploys a fresh one and the range is regenerated — craters and all.
