Saturn

Watch up to a million icy ring particles orbit Saturn under real physics, with sound.

Simulated live on your GPU in Saturn's measured gravity field (GM, J₂, J₄, J₆) and pulled on by eight moons; Mimas and the small ring moons are boosted in mass so their effects show. Rendered with WebGPU. The score is a synthesized drone built from Saturn's rotation and its moons' orbital frequencies, raised by whole octaves until you can hear them.
