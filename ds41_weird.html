r₀↟ · Afterbell

She lights the wick.

The flame rises straight, reaches the lowest chamber, and tilts. Six paths of light fall across the workshop floor.

One runs beneath the workbench. One climbs the hanging shears. One crosses the apprentice’s boot, pauses at the toe, and moves backward.

“Well,” she says. “That’s a fucking nuisance.”

She walks into the yard, holding the lantern high. In the nearest puddle, a small wave arrives from the wrong direction.

Tak. She taps the lantern with one fingernail. Toca.

The puddle turns.
