Cliffroad Cataclysm — the trailer, painted live

A trailer of about forty seconds, painted and scored live in the browser. It opens in near silence on a rider on a scarlet superbike crossing a calm coastal cliff road, drawn in indigo ink on bone paper with only the bike in colour. Title cards read: Nothing you are about to see was filmed. One road. One rider. Then the coast comes apart and colour floods in: rockfalls, derailed trains, exploding tankers, falling power pylons, a collapsing overpass and finally the sea itself, in fast cuts between cards reading Fourteen disasters and No brakes. After a slow, quiet wide shot captioned the sea wants its coast back, the cutting accelerates through two one-word cards, Run and Faster, until the title, Cliffroad Cataclysm, appears with the falling coastline visible inside its letters. The trailer contains bright flashes, fast cuts, camera shake and loud synthesised sound.
